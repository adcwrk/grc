# Evidence Classification

## Purpose

Classify source material and evidence records by type, sensitivity, scope, and provenance.

## Required Behavior

- Separate source material from accepted evidence.
- Never treat model-generated assumptions as evidence.
- Preserve source IDs and evidence IDs.
- Use `PUBLIC`, `INTERNAL`, `CONFIDENTIAL`, or `CUI` sensitivity labels.

## Output

- evidence metadata recommendations
- unresolved provenance questions
- source-to-evidence traceability notes
